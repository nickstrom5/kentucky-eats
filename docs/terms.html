Terms of use

The plain-language terms for the Kentucky Eats app and this website. Last updated 2026-11-06.

What the app is

Kentucky Eats is a free guide to restaurants in Kentucky. It is provided as is, for personal use, without charge and without warranties of any kind.

Check before you go

Restaurants open, close, change their hours and change their menus, and seasonal places keep seasons. Dishes and founding years are what each place or a named source said when we checked in fall 2026. We work to keep the lists right, but we can't promise that any listing is current or complete. Call the restaurant before you make the trip.

Inspections and public records

Inspection results in the app and on this site are Louisville Metro's own: the official score and, where Louisville gave one, its A, B or C grade, as published and dated. Elsewhere no result is shown, and each place links to its county's table on the state's public inspection portal. We compute no grade. For official records, use each agency's own site.
