Goetta, beer halls and a Beard finalist

meetNKY, Northern Kentucky's visitors bureau, notes that Food & Wine named Covington a top small U.S. city for food and drink in 2025. Its picks include the Anchor Grill, a 24-hour diner known for its GLT (goetta, lettuce and tomato), the Hofbräuhaus in Newport, the first in the U.S., Bouquet in MainStrasse Village, Libby's Southern Comfort, Frida 602 and The Gruff. The Baker's Table in Newport is where David Willocks cooks; the James Beard Foundation named him a Best Chef: Southeast finalist in 2026.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Covington (6), Newport (3).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated story, or a Kentucky tourism office's own page for it) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.
