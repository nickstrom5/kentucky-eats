Hot Browns

9 places for Louisville's open-faced turkey, bacon and Mornay sandwich, from the hotel that created it to the riffs on it. Each was checked in fall 2026.

Created at the Brown Hotel

Louisville Tourism tells the story: in 1926 the Brown Hotel's chef, Fred K. Schmidt, created an open-faced sandwich of turkey, bacon and Mornay sauce, and it was dubbed the Hot Brown. Kentucky Tourism sends you to the hotel's English Grill and J. Graham's Café for the original. Around town the dish turns up as Hot Brown fries and a Hot Brown burger at Sidebar at Whiskey Row, a Hot Brown pizza at Lou Lou and a vegetarian Veggie Brown at The Goose. In Frankfort, the 2025 Hot Brown Trail added a Hot Brown croissant at Andy's Artisan Bread, a Mexican-style one at Mi Fiesta and Hot Brown soup at the Kentucky Coffeetree Cafe.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Louisville (5), Frankfort (4).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated story, or a Kentucky tourism office's own page for it) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.
