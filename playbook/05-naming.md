# Kentucky Eats: name, icon and palette (final: Nick's decision 2026-11-07, "Yes, as proposed")

## Name
- **App Store name:** "Kentucky Eats: Restaurants" (26). **Subtitle:** "Hot Browns, Burgoo & Bourbon" (28). **Home Screen:** "Kentucky
  Eats" (13, fits under the icon).
- Checked 2026-11-06 with Apple's public search (itunes.apple.com/search): no app is named "Kentucky Eats", "Kentucky Table",
  "Bluegrass Table" or "Bluegrass Eats". "Kentucky Eats" brings up UK Athletics, KFC and Bojangles' apps, which share no words with it.
- Alternatives from the prompt: "Kentucky Table" and "Bluegrass Table" (both .coms free in Sept 2026 per the prompt; "bluegrass" pulls
  music apps). "Kentucky Eats" is the family's pattern and the best search fit; kentuckyeats.com and kyeats.com are parked (prompt).
- Avoided: "Unbridled Spirit" (state slogan), "Kentucky Proud" (Agriculture mark), "Kentucky Bourbon Trail" (the Kentucky Distillers'
  Association's mark; the app says "bourbon country"), Churchill Downs' Derby marks and twin spires, Derby-Pie, "Kentucky Fried".

## Icon
- **A (final, Nick 2026-11-07): a Hot Brown in its cast-iron skillet, from above** (golden Mornay, two bacon strips, two tomato slices, the
  handle level to the right) on the flag's navy. `scripts/icon-concepts.swift`, `scripts/make-brand.swift`.
- B: a bourbon ball with a pecan half (reads as a pot at 60 px). C: a Benedictine finger sandwich (reads clearly; pale green spread).
- All three pass `state-prompts/icon-style/check.swift`. No drumstick (KFC), no Derby imagery, no seal.

- Goldenrod #E6A817: fills only (pins, the title shadow), under navy text (5.8:1); never text on white (2.1:1).

- Header: a black four-plank horse-farm fence over two bluegrass hills on a navy baseline (Theme.swift `Stripes`, the site's band).
